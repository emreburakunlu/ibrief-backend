iBrief Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

iBrief is a private morning-brief app for iPhone, distributed as a TestFlight beta by Emre Burak Ünlü. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive or store any personal data from the app.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Calendar events (if you allow access): read on-device to build your brief. Never uploaded.
  • Location (if you allow access): used on-device to pick the area for weather. Never sent to the developer.
  • Watchlist, holdings, quantities and cost basis: stored on your device only.
  • Your Finnhub API key (if you add one): stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • AI summaries: generated on-device with Apple Foundation Models. Your content is not sent to any AI service.

Third-party services the app contacts

To fetch data, the app makes requests to the services below. These requests are made from your device, so those services can see your IP address and the request itself.

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): weather, and place-name search. Receives approximate coordinates or the place name you search for.
  • Finnhub (finnhub.io): stock quotes for the tickers on your watchlist, using your own API key.
  • Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko: market and crypto prices for the symbols you follow.
  • GitHub Pages (emreburakunlu.github.io): a generic daily feed (headlines, gold, indices, holidays) that is identical for everyone. It contains no personal data, and your holdings are never sent to it.

Each service has its own privacy policy, which applies to the data it receives.

Not financial advice

iBrief shows information and prompts to review. It does not give investment advice or recommendations to buy, sell or hold anything.

Retention and deletion

Everything is stored locally. Deleting the app removes all of it. The developer holds no copy.

Children

iBrief is not directed at children under 13.
